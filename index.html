Prototype notes

A working sketch of the proposed nav using a right-side drawer — matching the pattern already used elsewhere on the site. Toggle the menu and the active-page simulator to see how state, grouping, and the drawer behave. Resize below ~900px to preview the mobile pattern (drawer expands to full width).

Simulate active page:
Change 1

Transparent header that condenses on scroll

Over the hero, the top bar is invisible — light wordmark and outlined Menu button float against the sky. Past the hero, it fills with a cream backdrop, inverts to dark text, and shrinks slightly. Keeps the hero unbroken while staying useful on inner pages.

Change 2

Visible active state in drawer

Current page is marked with an accent dot beside the link, so users opening the drawer on an inner page immediately see where they are.

Change 3

Right-side drawer with grouped sections

Reuses the drawer pattern from the rest of the site. Sections stack vertically — Work · Company · Connect — making eight flat items easier to scan in a focused panel.

Change 4

Differentiated CTAs

"Contact" the link and "Book a Call" the action are now distinct intents — fewer redundant paths, clearer conversion.

Change 5

Readable menu icon + label

Replaces the dot-pattern icon with a clear "Menu" word + two-line glyph. Discoverability matters when the menu is the only way in.

Change 6

External-link affordance

Careers (Ashby) and Book a Call use ↗ instead of → so users know they'll leave the site or open a scheduler.
